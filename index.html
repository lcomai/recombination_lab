Mapping challenge

Model: crossover positions follow a smooth arm-specific rate curve with a zero-rate centromeric interval. A local inhibition rule supplies interference; it is an illustrative model, not a species-specific genetic map. Genotypes come from one randomly chosen chromatid per independent meiosis. Multiple exchanges can make the two-point estimate understate true map length. Recombination frequency approaches 50% for distant genes. The population recombination fraction does not exceed 50%, but a finite sample may contain more than 50% recombinants by chance. Only three-gene classroom samples are conditioned to COC no greater than 1. COC is shown only for three genes and uses observed double-recombinant gametes divided by the expectation from the two observed interval recombination fractions.
